1 + N: one measurement event, monetised N times

The core architectural bet: measure a recovered-materials batch once — with plant-floor IoT dMRV — then monetise that single measurement across N applications: EU Battery Passport, CBAM, OEM Scope 3, China ETS/CCER alignment, green finance, recycled-content claims. Certification is the product; the plant is the proof.

Infrastructure — the build

US$12B by 2030
120 plants × US$100M standard 50 ktpa modules (80 China + 40 ROW), scaling to US$100B / 800+ plants by 2050. Recycling is the only energy-transition infrastructure with guaranteed feedstock — every GWh sold is a dated delivery of raw material.

Materials — the prize

US$15B/yr by 2030
6 Mt EoL batteries a year (China 4 Mt, ROW 2 Mt) ≈ 150 kt LCE lithium, ~900 kt Cu/Al, plus Ni/Co from NCM. Every EV pack in the scrap pile holds ~US$1,500 of copper and aluminium at October 2026 prices (Cu US$14,500/t, +47% y/y).

Carbon — the costed liability

US$120B/yr today → US$1T/yr by 2035
OEM Scope 3, EU Battery Passport/CBAM, China's Ecological Environment Code + ETS absolute caps (Jan 2027). Compliance is the cheapest capital in the transition — because the alternative is paying the fine.

Insider observation: Western analysts undercount end-of-life volumes by ~7.5×. Our SOP point of difference: larger packs, heavier cycle rates, faster end-of-life. 2030 EoL is 6,000 kt, not the ~800 kt consensus.

Demand, carbon & the EoL wave

Demand trajectory sits below IEA NZE (5.5 TWh 2030) on our base case — validated against Arcane Capital and Circular Energy Storage — yet still compounds to 35 TWh by 2050. That is 21 Gt of embedded carbon needing measurement and settlement.

Regulatory drivers — the hard-compliance stack

Every driver below is a dated, costed obligation. They are not tailwinds to hope for; they are liabilities someone must settle. Cetacea is the bridge for international capital into this China-led architecture.

AUG 2026 — IN FORCE

China Ecological Environment Code 生态环境法典

First codified ecological-environment framework. Product carbon footprint becomes a compliance-grade data obligation across industrial products — the legal anchor for 生态工业产品 (Ecological Industrial Products).

JAN 2027 — SCHEDULED

China ETS moves to absolute caps

aluminium, cement soon after. Scope 3 supply-chain data becomes a settlement requirement — the demand engine for certified low-carbon recycled materials.

2026 — ACTIVE

CCER / voluntary carbon market restart

China's restarted VCM creates a domestic settlement instrument. ECRUs are designed as carbon insets — batch-linked, retired not resold — dual-tracked for Beijing Green Exchange and SG/EU settlement.

2027 — PHASE-IN

EU Battery Passport (due diligence + PCF)

Battery Regulation requires digital passports with embedded carbon data for EV and industrial batteries >2 kWh placed on the EU market. Recycled-content and PCF declarations become market-access conditions.

2026–2034 — RATCHET

EU CBAM definitive regime

Carbon cost embedded in imported goods moves from reporting to payment. Battery materials, aluminium and steel in scope — certified decarbonised materials carry a direct price advantage.

2026–2030 — PROGRAMME

China 15th Five-Year Plan 美丽中国

Zero-carbon factories as the transition engine room; ecological product value realisation 生态产品价值实现 elevated to national economic programme. The 1+N strategy rides this policy architecture directly.

China recycling paradox

~80% of licensed capacity is NCM-biased with broken LFP economics. Whalefall's GEN4 short-process hydromet recovers battery-grade Li2CO3 + FePO4 — the LFP gap is the entry point. First certified batch: Lishui dry-process, Q4 2026.
